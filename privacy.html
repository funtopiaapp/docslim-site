Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Applies to the DocSlim PDF app for iPhone and iPad by Funtopia App Company.

In short: DocSlim PDF does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. The app makes no network connections of its own. Your documents and photos stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. DocSlim PDF has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. We (Funtopia App Company) never receive your files or any information about how you use the app.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Files you import or create (PDFs, images, Office documents), stored in the app's private storage and protected by iOS file encryption.
  • Settings, and how many free saves you used today, kept on the device and in the iOS Keychain.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored, except today's free-save count kept in the Keychain, which iOS may retain so that the daily allowance is not restarted.

Device features the app uses

  • Camera: to scan documents, only when you choose to. Images stay on your device.
  • Photos: to open the photos you pick in Apple's photo picker (the app sees only those), and to save an image to your library when you tap Save to Photos. Your photos never leave your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode: to unlock the app when App lock is on. Apple handles the check; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Files and cloud drives: through Apple's Files app, only the files you pick or save. DocSlim itself does not connect to any cloud service.

Text recognition, document detection and all document processing run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

Purchases

The PRO unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Sharing

A file leaves your device only when you share, export, print or save it somewhere yourself.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
