Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026. Applies to the DocSlim PDF app for iPhone and iPad by Funtopia App Company.

In short: DocSlim PDF does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. The app makes no network connections of its own. Your documents, photos, signatures and profile details stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. DocSlim PDF has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. We (Funtopia App Company) never receive your files or any information about how you use the app.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Files you import or create (PDFs, images, Office documents), stored in the app's private storage and protected by iOS file encryption.
  • Profiles you create (for example name, date of birth, address, ID numbers, payment-card numbers), used only to fill forms when you ask. Profile details are encrypted with a key kept in the iOS Keychain. Card security codes (CVV) are never stored.
  • Signatures and ID photos you save, kept per profile.
  • Settings and trial status, kept on the device and in the iOS Keychain.

Deleting a profile erases its details, signatures and photos. Deleting the app removes everything it stored, except the trial record kept in the Keychain, which iOS may retain so that the free trial is not restarted.

Device features the app uses

  • Camera: to take ID photos and scan documents and cards, only when you choose to. Images stay on your device.
  • Photos: to save an image to your library when you tap Save to Photos. Photos you pick to import are chosen in Apple's picker; the app sees only those.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / passcode: to unlock the app when App lock is on, and before showing a saved card number. Apple handles the check; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Files and cloud drives: through Apple's Files app, only the files you pick or save. DocSlim itself does not connect to any cloud service.

Text recognition, photo background removal and all document processing run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

Purchases

The PRO unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Sharing

A file leaves your device only when you share, export, print or save it somewhere yourself.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
