Common questions

How do I make a PDF the exact size a portal asks for?
Open Slim to size, choose the PDF, tap a preset or type the limit in KB (for example 200), then Slim. DocSlim makes the file as close to the limit as it can without going over. If it can't get there, it offers black and white, splitting into parts, or removing pages.
How do I restore my PRO purchase?
Tap Get PRO at the bottom of the screen (or Menu, then Feature list, then Unlock), then tap Restore purchase. PRO is shared with your Family Sharing group.
Do my files get uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens on your device. Files leave it only when you share or save them yourself.
Where are my bookmarks?
Saving bookmarks makes a new copy of the PDF. Open that copy and tap Bookmarks at the top.
Each profile sees different files. Why?
Files belong to the profile that made them, so family members' documents stay separate. Switch profile with the avatar at the top.
How do I change the language?
Tap the globe button at the top of any screen. DocSlim is available in 15 languages.
